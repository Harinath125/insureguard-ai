Section 4. Premium rating

4.1 Base rate. The base rate is 0.22% of the insured value.

4.2 High-risk multiplier. Properties classified as high risk are rated with a multiplier of 1.40.
Note 4.2. The high-risk multiplier does not apply to properties with an approved sprinkler system. Such properties are rated with the standard-risk multiplier.

4.3 Standard-risk multiplier. All other properties are rated with a multiplier of 1.20.

4.4 Deductible credit. A deductible above Rs 1,00,000 receives a credit factor of 0.95.
