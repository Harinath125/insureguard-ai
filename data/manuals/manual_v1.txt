Version: v1
Effective date: 2025-01-01

Section 4. Premium rating

4.1 Base rate. The base rate is 0.20% of the insured value.

4.2 High-risk multiplier. Properties classified as high risk are rated with a multiplier of 1.50.

4.3 Standard-risk multiplier. All other properties are rated with a multiplier of 1.20.

4.4 Deductible credit. A deductible above Rs 50,000 receives a credit factor of 0.95.
